Why do some controls feel obvious while others shut people out? A design bootcamp in 2021 turned that question into my job.

EXIT 06 · REAR-VIEW Off the clock

Check the rear-view.

Off the clock, I'm usually behind a wheel or sketching one.

I spend my downtime around cars: reading up on builds, tracking new releases, and noticing how much good automotive design is the same discipline as good product design. Every dial, curve and control has to earn its place.

Cars taught me what great design feels like. You stop thinking about the machine, and you just drive.
