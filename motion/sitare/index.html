Astrology app · iOSUI & motion designFigma prototype

An app that moves like the night sky.

Sitare connects people with astrologers, kundli reports and matches. 162 screens across three apps, stitched together with smart animate, slides and quiet timers. The phone on the right runs the real prototype. Tap anything.

    Moments that play themselves

    The prototype hides small, timed scenes inside the flows: a request sliding in after two seconds, match cards reshuffling, a subscription screen cycling its backdrop. Each phone below runs one of them on a loop, with the timing pulled straight from the Figma file.

    Transition grammar

    Most of the app speaks one short sentence: 0.3 seconds, ease in and out. The slow springs are saved for the moments that should feel like a reveal.
